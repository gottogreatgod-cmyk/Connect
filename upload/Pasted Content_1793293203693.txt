# Template Factory — Business Family Trees × Tier Ladders

Every branch below is a **composable template spec**: same family tree, four tiers, each tier a complete site (not a stripped draft). Read as:

- **T1 Starter (low) → T2 Growth (mid) → T3 Business (high) → T4 Flagship (premium)**
- `+` means **adds to the tier above** — nothing is removed, so a T4 site contains its T1 skeleton.
- **DNA** = the dominant conversion path (one primary CTA, secondary actions support it).
- **Premium moment** = the flagship-layer integration that makes the tier feel elite.
- All fictional proof carries `DemoContentBadge`. All compositions inherit the **global baseline**.

**Global baseline (every template, every tier):** `SiteHeader` · `MobileNav` · `SiteFooter` · `StickyActionBar` · `Breadcrumb` · `SkipToContent` · `CookieConsent` + `ConsentGate` · `AnnouncementBar` · `ErrorBoundary` · `NotFound` · `FormField`/`InlineValidation`/`SuccessState` — then `ThemeToggle` + `BackToTop` from T2, `LanguageSwitcher` + `CommandPalette` from T3.

**Tier entitlement rule (from the mapping doc):** T1 = contact/call · NAP+map · 1 simple hero · minimal motion · basic events. T2 = booking/quote/order · reviews+FAQ · blog · 1–2 heroes · refined motion. T3 = multi-step intake · staff/resource booking · case studies+credentials · schema · 2–3 heroes · signature motion · optional isolated 3D. T4 = portal-grade workflows · accounts/loyalty/B2B · programmatic local/SEO · 3–4 heroes · art-directed motion · optional immersive 3D + AI · full funnel + dashboards · full localized journeys.
